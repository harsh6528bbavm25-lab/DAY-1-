# Generative AI for Aviation

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains my **Generative AI for Business** coursework focused on the application of Artificial Intelligence in the aviation industry.

The assignment explores how **Traditional AI, Generative AI, and different AI capabilities** can support airline and airport operations, passenger services, marketing, management, and decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand Traditional AI and Generative AI.
- Identify different AI capabilities used in aviation.
- Compare AI-generated outputs from ChatGPT and Google Gemini.
- Understand retrieved information vs generated information.
- Classify aviation tasks according to their AI capability.
- Select appropriate AI tools based on business requirements# DAY-1-
